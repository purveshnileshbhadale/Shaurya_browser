! Title: Aether seed list
! Description: A small, bundled starter list.
!
! This exists so that a fresh install is not defenceless. The real
! protection comes from EasyList, EasyPrivacy and the uBlock lists, which
! are ~112,000 rules downloaded on first run — this is about sixty, and it
! is deliberately not a substitute for them.
!
! Two kinds of rule live here, and the difference matters. A pure ad-serving
! host (doubleclick, adnxs, googlesyndication) is unqualified: it serves
! nothing but ads, including inside its own nested iframes, where a
! $third-party qualifier would stop matching. A domain that is also a
! company's real website (criteo.com, hotjar.com, taboola.com) is scoped
! $third-party, so its beacons are blocked on other people's sites while
! visiting the company itself still works. Getting that backwards is how a
! blocker starts breaking pages people meant to open.
!
! It covers only the largest and least ambiguous ad and tracking endpoints,
! the ones present on a large share of the web. Every rule here is a domain
! anchor on a host that exists to serve ads or collect analytics, so the
! false-positive risk of shipping them unconditionally is close to nil.
!
! Kept short on purpose: it is parsed on every cold start before anything
! else is available, and a long bundled list would both slow that down and
! rot faster than it could be updated.
